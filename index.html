Privacy Policy – Status Saver

Package: com.devlohya.statussaver · Last updated: 5 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Status Saver app ("the App") handles user information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we access

  • Storage / WhatsApp status folder. With your permission, the App reads the WhatsApp status media (images and videos) already stored on your device so you can view and save them. This media is processed only on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to any server.
  • Saved files. Media you choose to save is copied to your device gallery. Nothing leaves your device.
  • App preferences. Settings (for example, the selected folder) are stored locally on your device.

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, messages, or location, and we do not run our own servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and (where permitted) personalize ads. This data is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Data sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Disclaimer

Status Saver is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta. Please respect other people's privacy and copyright, and only save content you have permission to use.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
